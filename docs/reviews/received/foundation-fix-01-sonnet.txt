### One residual observation (not blocking)

`Earning.amountState`/`status` both declare an `'adjustment-review'` member (`lib/contracts/preview.ts`), presumably intended for the "surface a pending adjustment/review case" language in SOL3's correction text. In this implementation, a correction attempt against an already-approved/processing/paid earning is instead **rejected outright** (`EARNING_ADJUSTMENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED`) — the original ledger is left untouched and the caller is told to route the disagreement through the still-pending DEC-16 policy, but no record is ever actually created in the `'adjustment-review'` state; that enum member is currently unreachable dead code. This is a defensible reading of the packet (it does satisfy "do not silently rewrite... reject and surface... under unresolved production DEC-16"), and it is strictly safer than the original defect, so it is not a blocking finding — but it's worth a one-line contract note or removing the unused member before a consumer lane builds UI around an `'adjustment-review'` state that the service never actually produces.
